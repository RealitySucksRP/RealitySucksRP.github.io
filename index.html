About RealitySucksRP

I Learned By Building It, Breaking It, And Building It Again.

RealitySucksRP started with me learning FiveM hands-on. I build an idea, test it on a real server, find what feels wrong, then keep rebuilding until the gameplay makes sense. That process has grown from small UI and utility projects into complete systems with custom interfaces, framework support, server-side validation, multiplayer world state and a growing open-source layer.

I care about interfaces matching the experience, configuration being understandable, important actions being validated safely, and resources staying practical to maintain after release.
