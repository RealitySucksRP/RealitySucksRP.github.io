About RealitySucksRP

I Learned By Building It, Breaking It, And Building It Again.

RealitySucksRP started with me learning FiveM hands-on: building ideas, testing them, finding what did not work, and continuing until the gameplay felt right. What started with smaller projects has grown into original gameplay systems, complete interfaces, framework integrations, free open-source tools and premium resources.

I focus on what a feature does during actual gameplay—not only how the menu looks or how long the feature list is. Gameplay should have a purpose, interfaces should match the experience, configuration should be understandable, important actions should be validated safely, and the resource should remain practical to maintain after release.
